🛡️ Evasion & Countermeasures

Checklist and techniques for operational security and evidence destruction.

📋 OPSEC Checklist

  • 🔹 Use VPN + Tor for all communications
  • 🔹 Use encrypted messaging (Signal, Telegram)
  • 🔹 Regularly clear browser history & cookies
  • 🔹 Use burner phones and SIM cards
  • 🔹 Avoid reusing usernames or emails
  • 🔹 Use cryptocurrency mixers for payments
  • 🔹 Disable location services on devices
  • 🔹 Use disk encryption for all storage

🗑️ Evidence Destruction

  • 🔹 Physically destroy SIM cards after use
  • 🔹 Wipe hard drives with multiple passes
  • 🔹 Shred physical documents
  • 🔹 Use secure deletion tools (e.g., BleachBit)
  • 🔹 Destroy burner phones physically
  • 🔹 Delete all online accounts after campaign
  • 🔹 Use decoy documents to mislead investigators
  • 🔹 Regularly change encryption keys
